## Why

The August 1, 2026 website-reveal countdown has completed. Keeping an expired
countdown and future-tense launch copy on the canonical landing makes the public
project appear stale and obscures the infrastructure that is already visible.

Some product, NFC, and terminal labels also imply private implementation evidence
that is not present in the public repository set.

## What Changes

- Remove the completed launch date, countdown UI, and countdown JavaScript.
- Lead with the multi-layer Independent Protocol Infrastructure architecture.
- Preserve direct links to public testnet services.
- Replace unsupported implementation-status wording with precise architecture,
  research, model-layer, and public-endpoint labels.
- Keep the existing responsive design, official branding, and social metadata.

## Non-goals

- No claim that node, wallet signing, NFC, terminal, checkout, or payment code is
  implemented when it is not public.
- No change to DNS, service deployment, protocol behavior, or account handling.
